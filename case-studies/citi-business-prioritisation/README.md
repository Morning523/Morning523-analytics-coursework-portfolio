# Prioritising Four Business Cases at Citi

## Brief

An individual case exercise set around a real investment bank (Citi), structured as a Business Improvement Project (BIP)-style brief: four possible workstreams were on the table — P&L attribution audit remediation, structured notes growth, equity derivatives UI/UX, and valuation/risk AI — and the task was to decide where to focus first and how to sequence investment decisions, using only public information.

## Approach

Built on publicly available sources only (regulatory orders, press releases, Citi's own quarterly results) — no confidential Citi data was used or implied anywhere in this work. Every claim is tagged **CONFIRMED** (sourced), **INFERENCE** (reasoned from confirmed facts), **HYPOTHESIS** (untested, to be validated), or **UNKNOWN** (explicitly flagged as a gap), so the reader can see exactly what is evidence versus judgement.

For each of the four workstreams: the business problem, the commercial value at stake, the critical unknown that blocks a decision, and a concrete first action — followed by a 2x2 prioritisation (lead now vs. scope first) and a 3-week plan to move from brief to decision gate.

## Key output

- **Lead now:** P&L attribution (risk/audit triage) and structured notes (commercial growth discovery) — both have enough public signal to act immediately.
- **Scope first:** equity derivatives UI/UX and valuation/risk AI — both need a named user/task and a defined capability gap before any investment case can be built.
- A recommendation to keep the four business cases separate, and only coordinate across them where a dependency is proven to change value (not by default).
